Voice Infra runs voice agents that call your customers, answer your business
number, and talk to people in the browser and on WhatsApp. The same API key
also gives you the speech models underneath: text to speech, speech to text and
a language model, each with its own API.

Agents speak and understand Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu,
Kannada and Malayalam, and the Hinglish that most real calls are in.

## Pricing

Agent minutes cost ₹5 pay as you go, ₹3 with a ₹50,000 monthly commitment, or
₹1 with a ₹1,00,000 monthly commitment. Every company starts on pay as you go.
On the ₹1 plan, call insights (₹0.20 a minute, on by default) and our phone
line (₹0.30 a minute, if you use it) are added on top. Prices exclude GST.

[Billing & tiers](/general/tiers) explains each plan and how a call is billed.
The speech APIs are priced on their own; see the
[Speech API](/v2/speech#languages-and-prices) and
[language model](/v2/llm) pages.

## Your first call

Open the [console](https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co), go to **Developers**
and press **Create key**. The first time, the console asks for your work email
and sends you a confirmation link. The key is shown once, so put it in your
secret manager.

Create an agent:

```bash
curl -X POST https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co/v2/agents \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Order confirmation",
    "system_prompt": "You are Ashu from Acme. Confirm the order and ask whether the delivery address is unchanged. Keep replies short. When the customer is done, thank them and end the call.",
    "first_message": "नमस्ते, मैं Acme से आशु बोल रहा हूँ।",
    "voice": { "voice_id": "ashu", "language": "hi-IN" },
    "language": "hi-IN"
  }'
```

Then call a phone you are allowed to call, with the `id` you got back:

```bash
curl -X POST https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co/v2/calls \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "agent_id": "agt_01JZQ8F3K7M2N5P9R4T6V8W0XZ", "to": "+919876543210" }'
```

The [Quickstart](/v2/quickstart) has both requests in Python and Node, and the
webhook that tells you how the call went.
