## Post-call analytics everywhere

Every call on every tier can get an AI summary and a QA score. The capability
is part of
[every tier](/general/tiers#one-stack-on-every-tier). In the v2 API, turn on
[`analysis`](/v2/analysis) on an agent: the result is on the call, at
`GET /v2/calls/{id}/analysis` and in the `call.processed` webhook, and it can
pick the follow-up message template to send after the call.

## Warm transfer everywhere

Transfer to a human is not available yet, and it has no date. The plan is for
an agent on any tier to hand a call to a person at a number you set per agent.
A call that ends this way will report
[`ended_reason: transferred`](/v2/calls#ended-reasons).

## Model APIs

The models behind our calls also work on their own, for teams that want them
without the telephony. [Text to speech](/v2/tts-quickstart),
[speech to text](/v2/stt-quickstart) in ten Indian languages, over HTTP or a
WebSocket, and [offline transcription](/v2/transcriptions) are live today. The
call-tuned LLM comes next. Each one takes the same `sk_live_` key as the rest
of v2, and the [Speech API](/v2/speech) page lists them on one screen.

## New TTS languages

Gujarati is on the speech endpoint today, in beta. Five more languages follow
over the next two months: Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Kannada and Bengali. With
Gujarati, these six are the mother tongue of 429 million people, and most of
them can't be reached in Hindi at all. We announce each date as the language
clears evaluation. The [language roadmap](/v2/tts-languages) has the full story
and the data.

## Next-generation TTS

The next revision of the voice engine has lower first-audio latency and sounds
more natural on Hindi and code-switched speech. It replaces the engine under
every tier once it clears our quality bar. The API does not change, so your
calls simply start sounding better.

## Orchestration platform

The orchestration platform puts node graphs on a canvas, so you can build, test
and version call flows visually. These are the same graphs that node-based
agents run on every tier, but your ops team can author them instead of your
engineers. Validation runs as you draw, and every published version is
immutable, so a live campaign never runs an unreviewed edit.

## New voices

The catalogue grows every month from August 2026. New voices appear in the
[voice gallery](/v2/voices) as they clear evaluation. Listen to a new voice in
Agent Studio before you switch, because the choice of voice moves
answer-through rates more than prompt wording does.

## Channels

Calls will connect to other channels, starting with email follow-ups triggered
by call outcomes ("summary of what we agreed", "payment link after a confirmed
order"). Other channels come after that. The design has the call outcome
trigger each follow-up, so nobody has to send it by hand.
