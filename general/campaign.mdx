<Note>
This guide follows the **Campaigns** page in the console. To run campaigns from
your own code, use the [Campaigns API](/v2/campaigns).
</Note>

## Overview

A campaign calls a list of people with one of your agents. You choose when it
may call, upload the list and press **Start**. It works through the list inside
your calling hours, tries again when nobody picks up, and shows you each result
as it comes in. The example on this page is a round of
[payment reminders](https://voice.miraiminds.co/use-cases/payment-reminders).

## Before you start

- **Finish the agent first.** A campaign keeps the agent exactly as it is when
  you create the campaign. Changes you make to the agent later don't reach it.
- **Have your list ready** as a CSV file with a header row. An export from your
  CRM or a spreadsheet is fine.
- **Check your wallet.** You pay only for calls that are answered, by a person
  or a voicemail. If the wallet runs out, the campaign pauses until you top up.

## 1. Open Campaigns

In the console, open **Campaigns** in the sidebar. Your campaigns are listed
newest first, with a badge on any that are dialling, paused or still a draft.
**How it works** plays a three-minute guide to this page.

Press **New campaign**.

![The Campaigns page: four campaigns in a list, one dialling and one paused, with the How it works, Do-not-call list and New campaign buttons at the top right](/images/campaign-integration/campaign-list.png)

## 2. Name it and pick the agent

- **Name**: something you'll recognise later, like "EMI reminders · October".
- **Agent**: the agent that speaks on every call.
- **Calls come from**: the number people see when their phone rings. Pick one
  of your own numbers or pools (you add them under **Phone numbers**). If you
  leave it alone, the campaign uses the agent's own number, then your default
  pool, then your own number. The Mirai shared line is offered only while your
  workspace has no number of its own. The line under the picker tells you which
  one the campaign will use.
- **Timezone**: the dates and calling hours are read in this timezone.

There is nothing to choose under **Rate**. The campaign is charged at your
workspace's rate, the same as your other calls.

![The top of the New campaign form: Name, Agent, Calls come from set to the Mirai shared line, Timezone set to Asia/Kolkata, and the Rate line](/images/campaign-integration/new-campaign-basics.png)

## 3. Choose when to call

Set the **First day** and the **Last day**. Then set the **Calling hours**. You
start with one window. Press **Add a window** for another, up to four a day.

Nothing is dialled outside these windows. If you start the campaign at night,
it waits for the next window. A retry that falls due outside the hours waits
too.

![When to call: first and last day, and two calling windows, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM](/images/campaign-integration/when-to-call.png)

## 4. Set the pace, retries and limits

- **Calls at once** is how many conversations run at the same time, from 1 to
  50. When one ends, the next person on the list is called. Under the field the
  form works out the pace for you: at 5 at once, about 450 attempts an hour. If
  you call from your own number, it also tells you when that is more than one
  number should carry, because a busy number is more likely to be marked as
  spam.
- **Retries** and **Retry after (minutes)** decide what happens when nobody
  answers, the line is busy or the call fails. The campaign tries again after
  that gap, inside your calling hours. A call answered by a person or a
  voicemail is never retried.
- **Skip numbers already reached in an earlier campaign** leaves out anyone who
  answered, in person or by voicemail, in any of your earlier campaigns.
  Numbers that only rang out are still called.
- **Max call length (seconds)** ends a call that runs too long. Leave it empty
  to use the agent's own limit.
- **Budget (₹)** caps what this campaign can spend. When it is reached, the
  campaign pauses. Leave it empty for no cap.
- **Webhook URL** is optional. Add an `https://` address and we send each
  call's events there. Every call ends with one event that carries the
  transcript, the result and the contact's extra info, so your system doesn't
  have to fetch anything. See [webhooks](/v2/campaigns#webhooks).

![Pacing and retries: 5 calls at once with the pace line, 2 retries 60 minutes apart, the Skip numbers box ticked, a 300-second max call length, a ₹5,000 budget and a webhook URL](/images/campaign-integration/pacing-and-retries.png)

## 5. Add your contacts

### Download the template

Under the upload, the form lists the columns your agent reads, each marked
**Required** or **Optional**. Click **Download template** for a CSV with those
columns and a second row that explains each one. You can leave that row in when
you upload; it is skipped.

![The Contacts section: the file upload, and the EMI reminders agent's columns, phone, customer_name and emi_due required and due_date optional, with the Download template button](/images/campaign-integration/contacts-template.png)

### Fill in your contacts

One row per person, with the header row on top. Fill every required column.
The phone number can be in any column, and a ten-digit Indian mobile number gets
`+91` added for you. If you use Excel, format the phone column as Text before
you type the numbers, so Excel doesn't change them.

### Upload the file, or the one you already have

Press **Choose File** and pick your file. You can also paste the rows into the
box under it.

You don't have to start from the template. Upload your own export and open
**Columns** in the preview: each column of your file can be the phone number,
one of the agent's variables, extra info, or left out. We match them for you
(a "Name" column for `customer_name`, for example), and you can change any
match. Your changes are remembered for that agent in this browser, so next
month's export from the same system is matched for you.

![Columns: Mobile is the phone number, Name fills customer_name, EMI Due fills emi_due, and Branch is extra info that isn't given to the agent](/images/campaign-integration/contacts-columns.png)

### Check the preview

The preview shows how many numbers are ready, how many duplicates were removed,
and the first rows as they will be sent. It lists every row that can't be used,
with the line number and the reason. Those rows are left out and the rest go
ahead. All of this happens before anything is created.

Extra info is kept with each contact: it shows in the contacts list, the CSV
report and your webhooks, and the agent never sees it. Up to 16 columns can be
extra info.

![The preview: 6 numbers ready, 1 duplicate removed and 2 unusable rows with their reasons, above the Cancel and Create campaign buttons](/images/campaign-integration/contacts-preview.png)

## 6. Create it, then start it

Press **Create campaign**. The campaign is saved as a draft and its page opens.
Nothing is dialled yet, so you can check it first.

![The new campaign as a draft: 6 contacts, all waiting, with the Duplicate, Archive and Start buttons](/images/campaign-integration/campaign-draft.png)

When you're ready, press **Start**. The campaign dials only inside your calling
hours. If your wallet covers only part of the list, the page tells you how many
contacts it can pay for. The rest wait as pending, and they are called once you
top up.

<Tip>
Start with ten or twenty people. Listen to the first calls, then raise the pace.
</Tip>

## While it runs

![A running campaign: 6 contacts, 1 connected, 1 not reached, 4 waiting and ₹11.08 spent, with each contact's status, tries, last call and result](/images/campaign-integration/campaign-running.png)

The numbers at the top count who was connected, who wasn't reached and who is
still waiting, and what the campaign has spent so far. The bar under them splits
the list by status.

The **Contacts** tab has one row per person: status, tries, the last call and
its length, and the result your agent collected. Your extra info shows under
the name. Press the play button on a row to hear the recording and read the
transcript. A contact waiting for a retry shows when it will be tried again.

You can search the list, filter it by status, or use **Filters** to find people
by when they were last called, call length, result, tries, or any column from
your upload. The **⋯** menu on a row lets you call that person again, add them
to your do-not-call list, copy the number, or take someone who hasn't been
called yet off the list.

- **Pause** lets calls in progress finish and starts no new ones. Press
  **Resume** to carry on.
- The campaign also pauses itself when the wallet can't cover the next call, or
  when it reaches its budget. The page says which. Top up, or raise the budget
  under **Settings**, then press **Resume**.
- **Stop** ends the campaign for good. Calls in progress finish, nobody else is
  called, and a stopped campaign can't be resumed.
- **Download CSV** gives you one row per contact: your own columns (extra info
  included), the status and tries, and for the last call its length, how it
  ended, its cost, the result, a summary, the transcript and a recording link.

## Insights and settings

The **Insights** tab is the campaign's report. It fills in after the first
calls. The strip at the top jumps between its pages, from the summary and the
funnel to who to call back and when to dial next time. You can print it or save
it as a PDF, or press **Share** for a link anyone can open without signing in.
Recordings stay out of a shared report unless you tick **Include call
recordings**.

![The Insights tab of a finished campaign: Refresh, Print or PDF, Download CSV and Share above the strip of report pages, then the summary of EMI reminders · October with 41 conversations, 20 promised to pay (its most common result), ₹320.18 spent and 1h 00m of talk time](/images/campaign-integration/campaign-insights.png)

The **Settings** tab shows everything the campaign was set up with.

![The Settings tab: agent, rate, calling hours, dates, timezone, calls at once, retries, numbers reached before, max call length, budget, spent, webhook and created, with the Edit settings button](/images/campaign-integration/campaign-settings.png)

Press **Edit settings** to change them. What you can change depends on where the
campaign is:

- **Draft or paused**: everything except the agent, the timezone and the list.
- **Dialling**: only calls at once and calling hours. They apply straight away.
  Pause it to change the rest.
- **Finished**: nothing. Duplicate it to run it again with other settings.

## Run it again

- **Duplicate** copies the settings and the whole list into a new draft. Every
  number starts fresh as pending. Change anything before you create the copy.
- **New campaign from these**: on the **Contacts** tab, filter the list (for
  example, everyone who wasn't reached), then press **New campaign from these**.
  The new campaign gets just those people and the same settings.
- **Archive** takes a campaign off your list. Its calls, results and report are
  kept, and you can restore it at any time. A campaign can't be archived while
  it is dialling.
- The **Do-not-call list**, on the Campaigns page, holds numbers that no
  campaign in your workspace will ever dial, whatever a new file says.

## Use the API instead

Everything on this page is also in the API. See [Campaigns](/v2/campaigns) to
create a campaign with its contacts, add more contacts, start, pause, resume or
stop it, and read the report.

To send extra info through the API, put it in each contact's `metadata`. See
[Your own fields](/v2/campaigns#your-own-fields-metadata).

Each contact can also get its own voice and languages, through the API or with
`call_settings.*` columns in the CSV. See
[Voice and language per contact](/v2/campaigns#voice-and-language-per-contact).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Nothing in your list fills `customer_name`" | Open **Columns** and pick the column that holds it, or add it to your file. The template has every column the agent needs. |
| A row says "too short — add the country code" | Write the number with its country code, like `+919876543210`. Only Indian mobile numbers get `+91` added for you. |
| A row says "`emi_due` is empty" | That agent needs the value on every row. Fill the cell, or the row is left out. |
| A row says "`emi_due` must be a number" | Write a plain number, like `4200`, with no currency sign or text. |
| Phone numbers look like `9.83E+09` | Excel changed them. Format the phone column as Text, then paste the numbers again. |
| The campaign started but nothing is dialling | Check the dates and calling hours under **Settings**, in the campaign's timezone. It waits for the next window. |
| The campaign paused by itself | Read the line under its name. Top up your wallet, or raise the budget, then press **Resume**. |
| "Campaigns aren't switched on here yet" | Campaigns aren't turned on for your workspace. Ask us and we'll switch them on. |

## Good practice

- Set a budget on every campaign. It limits the cost of a mistake in the list,
  like a file uploaded twice.
- Call only people who have agreed to hear from you, and keep your do-not-call
  list up to date.
- Follow the rules for automated calls in the places you call.
