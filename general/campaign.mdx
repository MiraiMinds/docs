## Overview

A voice campaign places automated outbound calls to a list of contacts. You can load the contacts by uploading a CSV file, or send them from your existing systems through the API.

## Setup Guide

### Step 1: Access Campaign Creation

1. **Navigate to Campaigns Page**

   Go to the **Campaigns** section in your Voice Agents dashboard.

   ![Campaign dashboard](/images/campaign-integration/image_1.png)

2. **Create New Campaign**

   Click the **"Create Campaign"** button to start the setup process.

   ![Create campaign button](/images/campaign-integration/image_2.png)

### Step 2: Basic Campaign Configuration

3. **Enter Campaign Name**

   Give the campaign a name that describes its purpose.

   ![Campaign name field](/images/campaign-integration/image_3.png)

4. **Select Voice Assistant**

   Choose the voice assistant that will handle all calls for this campaign.

   ![Assistant selection](/images/campaign-integration/image_4.png)

5. **Configure Webhook Integration**

   Enable webhook integration if you want to send contact data from your platform through the API. With it enabled, CSV upload is optional.

   ![Webhook configuration](/images/campaign-integration/image_5.png)

### Step 3: Data Management Setup

#### Option A: CSV Data Upload

6. **Download CSV Template**

   Click **"Download CSV Template"** to get the correct format for your contact data.

   ![Download CSV template](/images/campaign-integration/image_6.png)

7. **Prepare Your Contact Data**

   Fill in the downloaded CSV template with your contacts. Complete every required field.

8. **Upload CSV File**

   Upload the completed CSV file using the file upload interface.

   ![CSV upload interface](/images/campaign-integration/image_7.png)

9. **Preview Contact Data**

   Check the uploaded data in the preview panel on the right before you continue.

   ![Data preview panel](/images/campaign-integration/image_8.png)

#### Option B: API Integration

With webhook integration enabled, your platform can send contact data directly through API calls, so you don't need to upload a CSV file by hand.

### Step 4: Schedule Configuration

10. **Set Campaign Dates**

    Set the campaign's start and end dates:

    - **Start Date**: Select when the campaign should begin
    - **End Date**: Choose when the campaign should conclude

    ![Date selection interface](/images/campaign-integration/image_9.png)

11. **Configure Timezone**

    Select the timezone for your campaign so that calls go out at the right local time.

    ![Timezone configuration](/images/campaign-integration/image_10.png)

12. **Set Operating Hours**

    Define your campaign's daily calling window:

    - **Start Time**: When calls can begin each day
    - **End Time**: When calls should stop each day

    ![Operating hours configuration](/images/campaign-integration/image_11.png)

### Step 5: Call Behavior Settings

13. **Configure Retry Logic**

    Set how the system handles unsuccessful call attempts:

    - **Retry Count**: Number of attempts to reach each contact
    - **Re-Attempt Period**: Time interval between retry attempts

    ![Retry configuration](/images/campaign-integration/image_12.png)
    ![Re-Attempt Configuration](/images/campaign-integration/image_13.png)

14. **Set Call Duration Limits**

    Configure call timing parameters:

    - **Maximum Call Duration**: Longest allowable call time
    - **Inactivity Period**: How long to wait with no response before ending the call

    ![Maximum call duration settings](/images/campaign-integration/image_14.png)
    ![Inactivity period settings](/images/campaign-integration/image_15.png)

### Step 6: Launch Campaign

15. **Create**

    When all the settings are done, click **"Create Campaign"**.

    ![Create campaign final step](/images/campaign-integration/image_16.png)

16. **Handle Data Validation**

    After clicking create, the system processes your data:

    - **Valid Data**: Successfully stored and included in the campaign
    - **Invalid/Missing Data**: Not stored but displayed in a validation modal

    The modal lists any invalid or missing entries. You can download them, correct them and upload them again.

### Step 7: Launch and Manage Campaign

17. **Launch Your Campaign**

    After you create the campaign, launch it from the campaign list. Click the **"Play"** button next to your campaign to start making calls.

    ![Campaign list with play button](/images/campaign-integration/image_17.png)

18. **Track Campaign Status**

    The **Campaign Status** page shows how the campaign is progressing, including:

    - Call completion rates
    - Success/failure statistics
    - Real-time campaign progress
    - Contact attempt history

    ![Campaign status tracking page](/images/campaign-integration/image_18.png)

19. **Copy Webhook API Endpoint**

    If you enabled webhook integration, click **"Copy Webhook"** to copy the endpoint URL that your platform sends data to through the API.

    ![Copy webhook endpoint button](/images/campaign-integration/image_19.png)

    Use this endpoint to programmatically add contacts to your campaign or trigger campaign events from your existing systems.

## What's Next?

Once your campaign is created and launched, you can:

- Track call success rates and campaign progress
- Pause, resume or modify running campaigns
- Review detailed campaign analytics and outcomes
- Download campaign performance reports

## Best Practices

### Data Preparation

- Test your CSV data with a small sample before uploading large datasets
- Format phone numbers correctly for the regions you are calling
- Fill in all required fields to reduce validation errors

### Campaign Optimization

- Set reasonable retry limits to avoid overwhelming contacts
- Configure appropriate operating hours to respect contact preferences
- Monitor initial performance and adjust settings as needed

### Compliance Considerations

- Make sure you have proper consent to contact every number in your campaign
- Respect do-not-call lists and opt-out requests
- Follow local regulations for automated calling
