A professional voice clone is a voice we build for your workspace from
recordings of one real person. You upload 30 minutes to 2 hours of them
talking, plus a short clip of them agreeing to it, and our team builds a voice
your agents can use on calls.

It's worth it when none of the [catalogue voices](/v2/voices) sounds like you:

- Your brand already has a voice, such as the person in your ads or the
  support lead your customers know by name.
- You need an accent the catalogue doesn't have, such as Australian English.
- You want a voice that is at home in your languages: Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu,
  or the Hindi and English mix your customers actually speak.

If a catalogue voice already sounds right, use it. It's ready today, and nobody
has to spend an afternoon talking to a microphone.

## Who can request one, and what it costs

Any workspace can send a request, from the console or the API. In the
[console](https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co), sign in with your work email
first; the page stays locked while you're only looking around. A workspace can
have five requests open at a time. Requests that are ready or not accepted
don't count towards that.

Custom voices come with a volume commitment on our Enterprise plan. See
[Billing & tiers](/general/tiers). Sending a request doesn't charge your
wallet: our team gets in touch to agree pricing with you before training
starts.

## Before you record

We build the voice from these recordings, so how they sound is how your agent
will sound.

### The speaker

- One speaker only. Every file must be the same person. If someone else talks
  during a session, for example to prompt the speaker with questions, cut them
  out before you upload.
- Speak the way the agent should sound on calls: conversational, at a natural
  pace, as if talking to a customer. A script read in a newsreader voice gets
  you a newsreader.
- Use the languages, and the mix, you want the voice to speak. If the speaker
  talks to customers in Hinglish, record Hinglish.

### The room and the microphone

- Record in a quiet room.
- Use the same microphone, at the same distance from the speaker, for every
  session.
- Send the audio as it was recorded: no music, no voices in the background,
  no heavy noise reduction and no effects. Trimming silences and false starts
  is fine.

### How much, and in what format

- Record 30 minutes at least and 2 hours at most. We add up the length of all
  your recordings, and a request can't be sent with less than 30 minutes or
  more than 2 hours. Trim long silences, so that the 30 minutes is the speaker
  talking.
- Split long sessions into several files if you like. Each file can be up to
  1 GB, with 3 GB in total.
- WAV or FLAC at 44.1 kHz or higher is best. MP3 at 192 kbps or better is
  fine. We also accept M4A, AAC, MP4, OGG, Opus and WebM.

## Consent

We only build a voice with the speaker's permission, and every request
carries it twice.

First, whoever sends the request confirms that the speaker has agreed, in
writing, to us building a voice from their recordings. You give your name and
your relationship to the speaker: yourself, an employee, a contractor or
someone else. Keep the written agreement where you can find it.

Second, the speaker records a short consent statement in their own voice. The
console shows the sentence to read and can record it in your browser, or you
can upload a recording of them reading it. The sentence is:

> My name is *speaker's name*. I agree to Mirai creating a synthetic version of
> my voice for *your company*. Today is *today's date*.

<Warning>
Never upload a voice from another platform, a celebrity's voice, or the voice
of anyone who hasn't agreed. We don't accept a request without the speaker's
consent.
</Warning>

The speaker can withdraw consent at any time. Delete the request, whatever its
status, and we delete its recordings and its consent clip. If we've already
built the voice, we take it down too. We keep the request's details, without
any audio, in our records for audit.

A voice is personal data. Make sure your agreement with the speaker says how
the voice will be used on your calls.

## Request a voice in the console

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a request">
    Open **Custom voices** in the sidebar, under **Workspace**, and click
    **Request a voice clone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us about the voice">
    Give the voice a name of up to 60 characters; this is how it appears in
    your list. Add the speaker's name and the languages the voice should speak:
    Hindi, English, Gujarati, Tamil or Telugu. Gender, accent (such as
    Australian English) and notes for our team are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm consent">
    Tick the consent statement, and add your name and your relationship to the
    speaker. Then have the speaker read the consent sentence, recorded in the
    browser or uploaded as a file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the recordings">
    Add the recording files. You need between 30 minutes and 2 hours in total.
    Until you send it, the request is a draft, so you can come back and add
    more later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Send the request. If something is missing, the console says what, for
    example "Add at least 30 minutes of recordings. You have 24 minutes so
    far."
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each status means

There's no fixed turnaround. Our team reviews the recordings and updates the
status as the request moves along. Anything we need you to know comes as a note
on the request.

| Console | `status` | What it means |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Draft | `draft` | You're still putting it together. You can edit it and add or remove recordings. |
| Sent | `submitted` | We have it, and it's waiting for our team. You can't edit it now. |
| In review | `in_review` | Our team is reviewing the recordings. We get in touch about pricing before training starts. |
| Training | `training` | We're building the voice. |
| Ready | `ready` | The voice is built. The request shows its `voice_id`. |
| Needs changes | `needs_changes` | We need something from you, and the note says what. Edit the request or its recordings, then send it again. |
| Not accepted | `rejected` | We can't build a voice from this request. The note says why. |

When a request is ready, our team gets in touch to switch your agents to the
new voice. Its `voice_id` is on the request so you can tell it apart from the
catalogue voices.

## Request a voice with the API

Base URL `https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co`. All endpoints require
`Authorization: Bearer sk_live_…`, and a request belongs to the workspace of
the key that created it.

```bash
export MIRAI_API_BASE="https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co"
export MIRAI_API_KEY="sk_live_YOUR_API_KEY"
```

| Method | Path | What it does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `POST` | `/v2/voice-clones` | Create a request. It starts as a `draft`. |
| `GET` | `/v2/voice-clones` | List your workspace's requests, newest first. |
| `GET` | `/v2/voice-clones/{id}` | Get one request, with its files. |
| `PATCH` | `/v2/voice-clones/{id}` | Change any of the fields you sent on create. Draft or needs changes only. |
| `DELETE` | `/v2/voice-clones/{id}` | Delete the request and all its audio, at any status. |
| `POST` | `/v2/voice-clones/{id}/files` | Register a recording or the consent clip before uploading it. |
| `PUT` | `/v2/voice-clones/{id}/files/{file_id}/chunks/{index}` | Upload one chunk of a file. |
| `POST` | `/v2/voice-clones/{id}/files/{file_id}/complete` | Finish a file once every chunk is in. |
| `DELETE` | `/v2/voice-clones/{id}/files/{file_id}` | Remove a file. Draft or needs changes only. |
| `POST` | `/v2/voice-clones/{id}/submit` | Send the request to our team. |

Files are large, so they go up in chunks of 16 MiB (16,777,216 bytes): you
register the file, upload its chunks, mark it complete, and submit the request
when everything is in.

### 1. Create a request

```bash
curl --fail-with-body -X POST "$MIRAI_API_BASE/v2/voice-clones" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MIRAI_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Priya (support)",
    "speaker_name": "Priya Sharma",
    "languages": ["hi", "en"],
    "gender": "female",
    "notes": "Warm and unhurried. She mixes Hindi and English on calls.",
    "consent": {
      "attested": true,
      "attested_by": "Rahul Mehta",
      "relationship": "employee"
    }
  }'
```

| Field | Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `name` | string | **Required.** The voice's display name, 1 to 60 characters. |
| `speaker_name` | string | **Required.** The person whose voice it is, 1 to 100 characters. |
| `languages` | array | **Required.** At least one of `hi`, `en`, `gu`, `ta` and `te`. |
| `gender` | string | Optional. `female`, `male` or `other`. |
| `accent` | string | Optional, up to 100 characters, such as `Australian English`. |
| `notes` | string | Optional notes for our team, up to 2,000 characters. |
| `consent.attested` | boolean | `true` confirms the speaker agreed in writing. It must be `true` before you can submit. |
| `consent.attested_by` | string | Your name, 1 to 100 characters. |
| `consent.relationship` | string | The speaker is `self`, an `employee`, a `contractor` or `other`. |

The response is `201 Created`. `attested_at` is the time we recorded your
confirmation.

```json
{
  "id": "vcl_01K8C2W7H4M9P3R6T1V5X8Z0QA",
  "object": "voice_clone",
  "status": "draft",
  "name": "Priya (support)",
  "speaker_name": "Priya Sharma",
  "languages": ["hi", "en"],
  "gender": "female",
  "accent": "",
  "notes": "Warm and unhurried. She mixes Hindi and English on calls.",
  "consent": {
    "attested": true,
    "attested_by": "Rahul Mehta",
    "relationship": "employee",
    "attested_at": "2026-10-21T17:10:00Z"
  },
  "sample_seconds": 0,
  "min_sample_seconds": 1800,
  "max_sample_seconds": 7200,
  "files": [],
  "voice_id": null,
  "status_note": "",
  "created_at": "2026-10-21T17:10:00Z",
  "updated_at": "2026-10-21T17:10:00Z",
  "submitted_at": null
}
```

```bash
export MIRAI_VCL_ID="vcl_01K8C2W7H4M9P3R6T1V5X8Z0QA"
```

### 2. Register a recording

Tell us about the file before you send it. `bytes` must be the file's exact
size. `duration_seconds` is its length; for a WAV file we read the length from
the file itself when you finish it, and for other formats we use the number you
send. `ffprobe -v error -show_entries format=duration -of csv=p=0 session-1.mp3`
prints it.

```bash
FILE=session-1.wav
BYTES=$(wc -c < "$FILE" | tr -d ' ')

curl --fail-with-body -X POST "$MIRAI_API_BASE/v2/voice-clones/$MIRAI_VCL_ID/files" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MIRAI_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d "{ \"kind\": \"sample\", \"filename\": \"$FILE\", \"bytes\": $BYTES,
        \"content_type\": \"audio/wav\", \"duration_seconds\": 1201.4 }"
```

`kind` is `sample` for a recording and `consent` for the consent clip. The
response is `201 Created`, and `chunks` says how many pieces to send:

```json
{
  "id": "vcf_01K8C2X3B6D9F2H5K8M1P4R7T0",
  "object": "voice_clone_file",
  "kind": "sample",
  "filename": "session-1.wav",
  "content_type": "audio/wav",
  "bytes": 211812352,
  "received_bytes": 0,
  "chunk_bytes": 16777216,
  "chunks": 13,
  "received_chunks": [],
  "duration_seconds": 1201.4,
  "duration_source": "client",
  "sha256": "",
  "status": "uploading",
  "created_at": "2026-10-21T17:12:30Z"
}
```

```bash
export MIRAI_VCF_ID="vcf_01K8C2X3B6D9F2H5K8M1P4R7T0"
```

### 3. Upload the chunks

Send chunk `0` to `chunks - 1` as raw bytes. Every chunk is exactly
`chunk_bytes` long except the last, which is whatever is left. Chunks can go in
any order, and sending one again overwrites it.

```bash
CHUNKS=13   # "chunks" from step 2
for i in $(seq 0 $((CHUNKS - 1))); do
  dd if="$FILE" bs=16777216 skip="$i" count=1 2>/dev/null |
  curl --fail-with-body -X PUT \
    "$MIRAI_API_BASE/v2/voice-clones/$MIRAI_VCL_ID/files/$MIRAI_VCF_ID/chunks/$i" \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer $MIRAI_API_KEY" \
    -H "Content-Type: application/octet-stream" \
    --data-binary @-
done
```

Each `PUT` answers `200` with the file, and `received_chunks` lists the chunks
we have so far.

### 4. Finish the file

```bash
curl --fail-with-body -X POST \
  "$MIRAI_API_BASE/v2/voice-clones/$MIRAI_VCL_ID/files/$MIRAI_VCF_ID/complete" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MIRAI_API_KEY"
```

We put the chunks together into one file. The response is the file with
`status` `complete` and its `sha256`. For a WAV file, `duration_seconds` now
comes from the file and `duration_source` is `header`. If any chunk is missing,
you get `409 incomplete` naming the missing chunks; send those and call
`complete` again.

Repeat steps 2 to 4 for each recording. The request's `sample_seconds` adds up
the length of every finished recording.

### 5. Add the consent clip

Upload the speaker reading the [consent sentence](#consent) the same way, with
`"kind": "consent"`.
A request holds one consent clip, up to 50 MB. To replace it, delete the old
one first.

```bash
FILE=consent.wav
BYTES=$(wc -c < "$FILE" | tr -d ' ')

curl --fail-with-body -X POST "$MIRAI_API_BASE/v2/voice-clones/$MIRAI_VCL_ID/files" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MIRAI_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d "{ \"kind\": \"consent\", \"filename\": \"$FILE\", \"bytes\": $BYTES,
        \"content_type\": \"audio/wav\", \"duration_seconds\": 10 }"
```

Then upload its chunks and finish it as in steps 3 and 4.

### 6. Submit

```bash
curl --fail-with-body -X POST "$MIRAI_API_BASE/v2/voice-clones/$MIRAI_VCL_ID/submit" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MIRAI_API_KEY"
```

The response is the request with `status` `submitted`. To submit, the request
needs `consent.attested` set to `true`, one finished consent clip, between
1,800 and 7,200 seconds of finished recordings, and no file still uploading.
If something is missing, you get `422 not_ready` with a message that says what:

```json title="422 Unprocessable Entity"
{
  "error": {
    "code": "not_ready",
    "message": "Add at least 30 minutes of recordings. You have 24 minutes so far."
  }
}
```

### Resume an upload

If an upload stops partway, get the request and look at the file in `files`:

```bash
curl --fail-with-body "$MIRAI_API_BASE/v2/voice-clones/$MIRAI_VCL_ID" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MIRAI_API_KEY"
```

`received_chunks` lists the chunks we already have. Send the others, then
finish the file. This Python function does both, and you can run it again
after any failure:

```python
import os, httpx

base = os.environ["MIRAI_API_BASE"]
auth = {"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.environ['MIRAI_API_KEY']}"}

def upload(vcl_id: str, file_id: str, local_path: str) -> dict:
    vc = httpx.get(f"{base}/v2/voice-clones/{vcl_id}", headers=auth, timeout=30).raise_for_status().json()
    f = next(x for x in vc["files"] if x["id"] == file_id)
    url = f"{base}/v2/voice-clones/{vcl_id}/files/{file_id}"
    have = set(f["received_chunks"])
    with open(local_path, "rb") as src:
        for i in range(f["chunks"]):
            if i in have:
                continue
            src.seek(i * f["chunk_bytes"])
            httpx.put(f"{url}/chunks/{i}", content=src.read(f["chunk_bytes"]),
                      headers={**auth, "Content-Type": "application/octet-stream"},
                      timeout=300).raise_for_status()
    return httpx.post(f"{url}/complete", headers=auth, timeout=300).raise_for_status().json()
```

### Follow the status

`GET /v2/voice-clones/{id}` returns the request with its `status` and our
`status_note`. `GET /v2/voice-clones` lists every request in your workspace as
`{"object": "list", "data": [...]}`, newest first. The status changes when our
team moves the request along, so there's no need to check often. When it is
`ready`, `voice_id` names your new voice, and we'll help you move your agents
onto it.

### Change or delete a request

While a request is `draft` or `needs_changes`, `PATCH /v2/voice-clones/{id}`
changes any of the fields from step 1; at any other status it answers
`409 not_editable`. Adding and removing files works in those two statuses
only. `DELETE /v2/voice-clones/{id}/files/{file_id}` answers
`{"id": "vcf_…", "object": "voice_clone_file", "deleted": true}`.

`DELETE /v2/voice-clones/{id}` works at any status. It deletes every recording
and the consent clip, and answers:

```json
{ "id": "vcl_01K8C2W7H4M9P3R6T1V5X8Z0QA", "object": "voice_clone", "deleted": true }
```

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Recordings per request | 30 minutes to 2 hours in total |
| File size | 1 GB per file, 3 GB per request |
| Files per request | 60 |
| Consent clip | One per request, up to 50 MB |
| Chunk size | 16 MiB (16,777,216 bytes); the last chunk is the remainder |
| Open requests per workspace | 5 (ready and not accepted requests don't count) |
| Languages | `hi`, `en`, `gu`, `ta`, `te` |
| File types | `.wav`, `.flac`, `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.aac`, `.mp4`, `.ogg`, `.opus`, `.webm` |

## Errors

Errors use the [standard envelope](/v2/errors).

| Status | `code` | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `400` | `invalid_request` | A field is missing or out of range. The message names it. |
| `400` | `bad_chunk` | A chunk is the wrong size. Every chunk but the last must be exactly `chunk_bytes`. |
| `404` | `not_found` | No such request or file in your workspace. |
| `409` | `limit_reached` | Your workspace already has five open requests, or the request already has 60 files, 3 GB of audio or a consent clip. |
| `409` | `not_editable` | The request has been sent, so it can't be changed. You can still delete it. |
| `409` | `incomplete` | You finished a file with chunks still missing. `error.missing_chunks` lists them. |
| `409` | `file_complete` | That file is already finished, so it takes no more chunks. |
| `409` | `assembling` | We're joining that file's chunks right now. Wait a moment, then check the file. |
| `413` | `file_too_large` | The file is over 1 GB, or the consent clip is over 50 MB. |
| `415` | `unsupported_file_type` | We can't take that file type. Send WAV, FLAC, MP3, M4A, AAC, MP4, OGG, Opus or WebM. |
| `422` | `not_ready` | The request isn't ready to send. The message says what's missing. |
| `507` | `insufficient_storage` | We can't take more audio right now. Try again later. |
