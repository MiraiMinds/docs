## Getting started

**How do I get a key?**
Create one in the [console](https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co) under
**Developers → Create key**. You get a secret key, a webhook secret and a
starting wallet balance immediately, and you can rotate or revoke the key on
the same page. If you do not have console access yet,
[talk to us](https://cal.com/srikrishna-pothel-y5vpeq).

**Is there a sandbox or test key?**
Not yet. There is no `sk_test_` and no simulated call. Every call is a real
call and costs real money, so test against your own phone with a small wallet
balance. The [webhook test vector](/v2/webhooks#test-vector) lets you build and
verify your handler without placing a call at all.

**Is there an SDK?**
Yes, for Python:

```bash
pip install --extra-index-url https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co/pypi/simple mirai-voice
```

The SDK builds in the rules you would otherwise have to work out yourself: an
`Idempotency-Key` on every call create so a retry never dials twice,
`Retry-After`-aware backoff, cursor iterators, and
`mirai.webhooks.verify()` over the raw body. It supports sync and async use and
both credential generations. The cURL, Python and Node examples on every page
are ready to copy and work just as well, because the API is deliberately small.

## Calls

**Why did `POST /v2/calls` return `202` and not `200`?**
Because the phone has not rung yet. `202` means queued. The outcome arrives by
[webhook](/v2/webhooks), or from [`GET /v2/calls/{id}`](/v2/calls#get-a-call).

**How long until the phone rings?**
Normally a couple of seconds after the `202`. If you are at your concurrency
ceiling, the call waits in `queued` until a slot frees up. We queue it instead
of rejecting it, so going over the ceiling never returns `429`.

**Can I do inbound calls?**
Not yet. The API places outbound calls only.

**Can I transfer a call to a human?**
Not yet. Transfer to a human is on the [roadmap](/general/roadmap), but no
agent can transfer a call today. Until it ships, have the agent tell the caller
that the team will call them back, and follow up from your own systems.

**Can I change the prompt or variables mid-call?**
The prompt and `variables` are fixed for a call. On a
[browser call](/v2/web-calls), your backend can still direct the agent while it
runs with a [live `instruction`](/v2/web-calls#live-control), close it with a
spoken goodbye, and let it wrap up on time with a
[timing policy](/v2/web-calls#timing-policy).

**Can I get the recording or transcript?**
Yes. Fetch them with [`GET /v2/calls/{id}/transcript`](/v2/calls#get-the-transcript)
and [`GET /v2/calls/{id}/recording`](/v2/calls#get-the-recording), or receive the
transcript in a webhook with [final results](/v2/webhooks#final-results). To
store no audio, create calls with `recording_enabled: false`. Retention is on
the [limits page](/v2/limits#data-retention).

**What happens if the customer does not answer?**
The call ends with `status: no_answer`, you get a `call.failed` webhook, and
there is no charge. v2 does not retry for you, so retry from your own dialler if
you want to.

When the carrier gives us no verdict at all, we report an unanswered call as
`failed` with `ended_reason: "no-media"` instead. Both outcomes are free and
both mean the call did not connect, so treat `no_answer`, `busy`, and `failed`
with `no-media` as one bucket in your retry logic. See
[ended reasons](/v2/calls#ended-reasons).

**How do I stop a call I just started?**
Use [`POST /v2/calls/{id}/abort`](/v2/calls#abort-a-call). It works while the
call is queued, while it is ringing and while it is live. A live call is hung up
mid-conversation. Only a call that has already ended returns `409`. Aborted
calls are never billed.

**Which languages work?**
The stack is tuned for Hindi and Indian English, including Hindi-English
code-switching, which is how most real calls sound. Write the prompt in the
language you want spoken, using Devanagari for Hindi. For other languages, ask
us before you build on them.

**Does the agent sound like a human?**
It sounds like a good phone voice. Callers usually work out that it is a machine
within a turn or two, so [telling them up front](/v2/limits#disclosure-that-it-is-an-ai)
costs you nothing, and it reduces early hang-ups.

## Billing

**What does a call cost?**
It depends on your plan. Every company starts on T5 at ₹5 a minute, pay as
you go. A ₹50,000 monthly commitment brings T3 at ₹3 a minute, and ₹1,00,000
brings T1 at ₹1 a minute for voice AI. On T1, call insights are on by default
at ₹0.20 a minute and can be turned off in Billing. Managed phone calling adds
your provider's configured cost; BYO telco, WhatsApp and browser calls have no
Mirai telco charge. Voice is billed in 10-second blocks, rounded up, with a
minimum of one block. On T3, a 96-second call costs ₹5.00 for voice and an
8-second call costs ₹0.50 for voice. See [Billing & tiers](/general/tiers).

**Am I charged for calls that do not connect?**
No. `no_answer`, `busy`, `failed` and `aborted` are free. `completed`,
`voicemail` and `timeout` are billed.

**Am I charged for a voicemail?**
Yes, because media went live and audio was generated. An 18-second voicemail is
two billed blocks, which is ₹1.00 on T3.

**What happens when I run out of credit?**
`POST /v2/calls` returns [`402 insufficient_balance`](/v2/wallet#402-insufficient-balance)
and nothing is dialled. Calls already in flight finish normally.

**How do I top up?**
Once your company has gone live, add credit from the **Billing** page of the
[console](https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co); before that, ask your Mirai
contact. Credits land as a `credit` row in
[`GET /v2/wallet/transactions`](/v2/wallet#list-transactions) and take effect
immediately.

**Which tier am I on?**
Your company's plan sets the tier. Every company starts on T5, pay as you go.
Complete **Go live** in the console and you can call today. Commit ₹50,000 a
month to move to T3, or ₹1,00,000 a month to move to T1. Every tier runs on the
same Mira stack, and the price is the only difference. See
[Billing & tiers](/general/tiers).

## Webhooks

**My signature never verifies. Why?**
Almost always because you signed a re-serialised body. Sign the **raw bytes**;
see [raw body](/v2/webhooks#raw-body). The second most common cause is signing
only the body. The signed string is `"<t>.<raw_body>"`, so it includes the
timestamp and the dot.

**Do you retry failed deliveries?**
Yes. We make six attempts with exponential backoff over roughly 36 minutes, then
move the event to the dead-letter queue. Any `2xx` stops the retries. See
[retries](/v2/webhooks#retries).

**Can events arrive out of order or twice?**
Yes to both. Dedupe on `event.id` and order your state machine by
`data.call.status`, not by arrival time.

**Can I have more than one webhook URL?**
One per call, set as `webhook_url` at create time. Fan out on your side.

**What if my endpoint is down for an hour?**
After about 36 minutes of retries, the event goes to the dead-letter queue, and
our ops team can replay it. You can also reconcile from
[`GET /v2/calls`](/v2/calls#list-calls), which is what the list endpoint is
for.

## Limits and compliance

**How many concurrent calls can I run?**
The pilot default is 5. It is a provisioning setting, so raising it needs no
code change. Ask us for more. See [Limits](/v2/limits).

**Do you enforce the 9am to 9pm calling window?**
No. Your scheduler enforces it, and we dial when you tell us to. See
[India calling rules](/v2/limits#india-calling-rules).

**Do you scrub DND numbers?**
No. The list you call is yours, and so is the obligation to scrub it.

**Do I have to tell people it is an AI?**
We strongly recommend it, and the disclosure belongs in `first_message`. You
need to confirm your own legal obligations.

## Operations

**What is your uptime target?**
We operate the API and the call path as a pilot service. Ask your account
contact for the current SLA before you put a revenue-critical flow behind it.

**Where does my data go?**
Media (audio, ASR, TTS and the model) runs in India. The control plane, which is
workflow orchestration and the API, runs in Europe. It is off the audio path,
so it adds no latency to what the caller hears.

**Something is broken. What do you need from me?**
Send the `call_id` (or `evt_id`), the UTC timestamp and what you expected to
happen. With those we can pull the whole workflow history.
