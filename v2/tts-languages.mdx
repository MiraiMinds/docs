Today the engine speaks **Hindi, English and the Hinglish** that most real
calls are in, plus **Gujarati, Tamil and Telugu**. **Kannada and Malayalam**
are in beta. **Marathi and Bengali** come next.

We announce each language's date as it clears evaluation. Everything on the
[roadmap](/general/roadmap) follows the same rule: dates are targets, and if a
date and quality conflict, quality always wins. A language ships when it sounds
like a person, even if that is later than the calendar says.

## Gujarati, Tamil and Telugu

**Live now.** Send Gujarati, Tamil or Telugu text and you get audio in that
language from the same [speech endpoint](/v2/tts-quickstart), with the same
voices and at the same price. You don't add a language parameter or any other
field, and you don't change any code, because the model reads the script you
send.

- **Send text in its own script**: Gujarati in ગુજરાતી, Tamil in தமிழ்,
  Telugu in తెలుగు. That is what the model reads.
- **English words stay in Latin script**, mixed in mid-sentence, exactly the
  way they already work in Hindi.
- **Speech recognition is live too.** Mira STT transcribes the same languages
  with `gu-IN`, `ta-IN` and `te-IN`. See [STT languages](/v2/stt-languages).

## Kannada and Malayalam

**Beta.** All six voices speak Kannada and Malayalam. It works the same way as
Gujarati, Tamil and Telugu: send the text in its own script and you get audio
in that language from the same [speech endpoint](/v2/tts-quickstart), at the
same price, with no language parameter. An agent whose `language` is `kn` or
`ml` speaks it on phone and web calls.

- **Send text in its own script**: Kannada in ಕನ್ನಡ, Malayalam in മലയാളം.
  English words stay in Latin script.
- **Where it works today**: phone and web calls, `POST /v1/audio/speech`, and
  the playground in the console. The streaming socket and realtime sessions
  come later.
- **Speech recognition is in preview.** Mira STT accepts `kn-IN` and `ml-IN`.
  See [STT languages](/v2/stt-languages).
- **Beta** means the voices are ready to try and we are still tuning them on
  real calls. Tell your account contact what you hear.

## Why these six

Gujarati, Tamil and Telugu are live, Kannada is in beta, and Marathi and
Bengali come next. Together these six languages are the mother tongue of **429 million people**,
roughly every third Indian. For most of them, Hindi is no substitute. The
census records how many speakers of each language also speak Hindi, and for
four of the six the answer is *almost none*.

**357 million people cannot be called in Hindi at all**, so a voice agent that
speaks only Hindi cannot reach them. Only 1.5% of Tamil speakers report
speaking Hindi; for Telugu it is 5.7%, for Kannada 4.7% and for Bengali 8.6%.

With Hindi and these six together, an agent can speak the mother tongue of
**about four in five Indians**.

are the most recent official figures. The next census is due in 2027.
</Note>

## What support means

- **Same endpoint, same request shape.** The
  [speech endpoint](/v2/tts-quickstart) does not change. You send text in the
  language's own script, code-switched with English the way real conversation
  is, and get audio back.
- **The same voices.** Every live language uses the voices in the
  [voice gallery](/v2/voices). You keep the voice you chose and change only
  the text.
- **Speech recognition alongside.** Gujarati, Tamil and Telugu are live in
  [speech to text](/v2/stt-languages) as well as in text to speech. Marathi,
  Kannada, Malayalam and Bengali are in preview there today.

## Get early access

Each language runs an alpha with a small set of customers before it goes
live. If calls in Marathi or Bengali matter to your business, tell
your account contact which one. We allocate alpha slots in the order customers
ask, and we tune the final voices on alpha customers' text.
