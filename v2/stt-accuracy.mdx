Every day we check a sample of each campaign's answered calls. We transcribe
the caller's side of the recording again, offline, and compare it word by word
with what live speech recognition heard during the call. The report tells you:

- how many of the caller's words were heard right, and how that has moved since
  the campaign began;
- how often your [keyterms](/v2/keyterms) were heard right;
- the words it keeps mishearing, what it heard instead, and calls you can
  listen to;
- which of those words to add as keyterms. Add one with a single request, or
  let the campaign add them itself.

## How it's measured

- We check answered campaign calls where the caller spoke at least three times
  and the recording was kept: up to 20 calls a campaign a day
  (`method.daily_cap` has the exact number), never the same call twice. Calls
  made with recording turned off can't be checked.
- The live transcript is compared with our own offline transcription of the
  caller's side of the recording. We fix each campaign's offline setup at the
  first call we check: the language, the agent's name and the opening of its
  prompt, and the keyterms the campaign had then. Words you add later don't
  change it, so every day is measured the same way.
- Only the caller's words count, inside the moments the caller was speaking.
  Case, punctuation and script are not errors: "hotel" and "होटल" are the same
  word. Numbers count the same however they are written, so "पांच सौ" matches
  "500". Fillers like "umm" are left out.
- It is a machine check, not a person listening. Both transcripts come from our
  own speech recognition, so a mistake both make is not counted, and the error
  rate reads lower than a person checking the same calls would find. Watch the
  trend more than the number.

Until checks are switched on for your workspace, the report's `status` is
`off`.

### Status

| `status` | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| `off` | Checks are switched off. Numbers already checked are still shown. |
| `measuring` | No call has been checked yet. |
| `not_enough_calls` | Fewer than 5 calls checked. The numbers are there but too few to trust. |
| `ready` | At least 5 calls checked. |

### Fields

Error rates (`wer`) are word error rates: the share of the caller's words that
live speech recognition got wrong, missed or added, from `0` to `1`. Words heard
right is `1 - wer`. A rate can go above `1` when the live transcript added many
words. Each day's rate pools all the words of that day's calls. Dates are India
dates (Asia/Kolkata).

| Field | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| `method.reference` | Always `offline`: what the live transcript was compared with. |
| `method.calls_scored` | Calls checked so far. |
| `method.since` | The first day with a checked call, `YYYY-MM-DD`. `null` before any. |
| `method.daily_cap` | The most calls checked for one campaign in a day. |
| `summary.first_wer`, `summary.latest_wer` | The error rate on the first and on the latest day with at least 5 checked calls. `null` until there is one. |
| `summary.calls_scored` | Calls checked. |
| `summary.ref_words` | Caller words compared. |
| `summary.keyterm_recall` | Of the times a caller said one of today's keyterms in the last 7 days, the share the live transcript got right, `0` to `1`. Terms are counted from before you added them too, so you can see the change. `null` when no caller said one. |
| `daily[]` | One row per day with checked calls, oldest first, up to 180 days: `date`, `calls`, `ref_words`, `wer`, `keyterm_recall`. |
| `keyterm_events[]` | Changes to the keyterms, oldest first: `at` (RFC 3339, India time), `term`, `action` (`add` or `remove`), `source` (`manual` for an edit, `suggestion` for an accepted suggestion, `auto` for [keep learning](#keep-learning)) and `scope` (`campaign` or `agent`). Put them on a chart beside `daily` to see what each change did. |
| `misheard[]` | Words live speech recognition got wrong in the last 14 days: `term`, `heard_as` (what it wrote instead, with a count; `""` means it wrote nothing), `calls`, `count`, `in_keyterms` (already on the list) and `example_call_ids` to listen to. |
| `suggestions[]` | Words worth adding as keyterms, from the last 14 days: `term`, `reason`, `confidence` (`0` to `1`), `calls`, `heard_as` and `spellings`. |
| `auto_learn` | Whether [keep learning](#keep-learning) is on. |

A word in `misheard` and `suggestions` is spelled the way the live transcript
writes it, because keyterms are matched as written. On a Hindi call that is
often Devanagari, even for an English or brand word. `term` is the spelling the
live transcript used when it heard the word right, else the spelling of our
offline transcript. `spellings` lists `term` first and then every other
spelling seen. A misheard word and its suggestion carry the same `term`.

## Read an agent's report

```http
GET /v2/agents/{id}/stt-accuracy
```

The same report for every campaign of the agent together. `in_keyterms` and
`keyterm_recall` use the agent's own keyterms, `keyterm_events` include its
campaigns' changes (with `scope: "campaign"`), and `auto_learn` is always
`false`: keep learning is set per campaign.

## Add a suggested word

```http
POST /v2/campaigns/{id}/stt-accuracy/suggestions/accept
```

Adds a word to the campaign's keyterms, after the ones it has. The campaign's
next call listens for it, running campaigns included.

| Field | Required | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `term` | yes | The word or phrase, usually a suggestion's `term`. It follows the [keyterm rules](/v2/keyterms#rules-and-limits). |
| `spellings` | no | Up to 10 more spellings of the same word to add with it. Send the suggestion's `spellings`. |
| `scope` | no | `campaign`, the default. `agent` is refused with `409 agent_scope_unsupported`: add the word to the agent with [`PATCH /v2/agents/{id}`](/v2/keyterms#set-them-on-an-agent) instead. |

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="cURL">

    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co/v2/campaigns/cmp_01K7QAC5N9P4R7S3T6V8W2X4YB/stt-accuracy/suggestions/accept \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_YOUR_API_KEY" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{ "term": "कोरमंगला", "spellings": ["कोरमंगला", "Koramangala"] }'
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Python">
    ```python

        added = httpx.post(
            f"{API}/v2/campaigns/cmp_01K7QAC5N9P4R7S3T6V8W2X4YB/stt-accuracy/suggestions/accept",
            headers=auth,
            json={"term": s["term"], "spellings": s["spellings"][:10]},
            timeout=30,
        ).raise_for_status().json()

    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Node.js">
    ```javascript

      const res = await fetch(
        `${API}/v2/campaigns/cmp_01K7QAC5N9P4R7S3T6V8W2X4YB/stt-accuracy/suggestions/accept`,
        {
          method: "POST",
          headers: { ...auth, "Content-Type": "application/json" },
          body: JSON.stringify({ term: s.term, spellings: s.spellings.slice(0, 10) }),
        }
      );
      if (!res.ok) throw new Error(JSON.stringify(await res.json()));
    }
    ```
  </Tab>

```json title="200 OK"
{
  "term": "कोरमंगला",
  "added": true,
  "scope": "campaign",
  "keyterms": ["Gold Plus", "कोरमंगला", "Koramangala"]
}
```

`keyterms` is the campaign's whole list after the change. A word the list
already has, ignoring case, is not added twice: you get `200` with
`added: false`. The change shows in `keyterm_events` with
`source: "suggestion"`.

| Status | `error.code` | When |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `400` | `invalid_request` | No `term`, more than 10 `spellings`, a `scope` other than `campaign` or `agent`, or a word that breaks the keyterm rules. |
| `404` | `not_found` | No such campaign in your workspace. |
| `409` | `agent_scope_unsupported` | `scope` is `agent`. |
| `409` | `keyterms_full` | The campaign would pass 100 keyterms. Nothing was added. Remove one with [`PATCH /v2/campaigns/{id}`](/v2/campaigns#words-to-catch) first. |
| `409` | `campaign_finished` | The campaign is stopped or completed. Add the word to its agent instead. |

The messages on this route are plain sentences you can show to people as they
are.

## Keep learning

Turn on keep learning and the campaign adds the words it is surest about by
itself:

```bash
curl -X PATCH https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co/v2/campaigns/cmp_01K7QAC5N9P4R7S3T6V8W2X4YB \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "keyterms_auto_learn": true }'
```

- A suggestion is added when its `confidence` is at least `0.8` and it was
  seen in at least 3 calls, with all its spellings.
- At most 5 words are added a day.
- Each addition shows in `keyterm_events` with `source: "auto"`, so you can see
  what changed and when. Remove a word you don't want with a normal
  [`PATCH`](/v2/campaigns#words-to-catch).
- It is off unless you turn it on. You can set `keyterms_auto_learn` on create
  or with `PATCH`, while the campaign is running too. A duplicated campaign
  starts with it off.

## In the console

Open a campaign's **Insights** tab. **How well your agent hears** shows the
share of words heard right, how it moved since the campaign began, a
day-by-day line with a mark on each day words were added, and **Words it gets
wrong**. Press **Add** beside a word to add it to the campaign's words to
catch, or **Listen** to hear the calls it came from. The **Keep learning**
switch is on the same card.

For an agent, **Agent Studio → Behavior → How well it hears** shows the same
check across its campaigns, under [Words to catch](/v2/keyterms).

## Errors

Both reports answer `404 not_found` for a campaign or agent that is not in your
workspace, and `503 stt_accuracy_unavailable` where accuracy checks are not
available at all. See [errors](/v2/errors#campaigns).
