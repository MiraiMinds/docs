A **concurrent channel** is one call or Realtime session in progress at a
time. Your channels are shared by everything your account runs: agent calls on
every channel (phone, browser, WhatsApp and text), campaign calls, incoming
calls and [Realtime API](/v2/realtime) sessions.

A channel is one call or Realtime session in progress at a time, across your whole account: agent calls on every channel (phone, browser, WhatsApp and campaigns) and Realtime sessions. Every account includes 2 channels. Each extra channel costs ₹1,000 a month, paid from your wallet when you add it and again each month it renews. If your wallet cannot cover a renewal, that channel lapses at the end of its month; calls in progress are never cut. All prices exclude GST.

## What uses a channel

A call holds a channel from the moment we start placing it until it ends,
including while the phone rings. A Realtime session holds one from the moment it
is admitted until the socket closes. When a call or session ends, its channel is
free for the next one straight away.

You can see your channels, and how many are in use, in the
[console](https://sandbox.voice.miraiminds.co) under **Billing → Plan tier**.

## At the limit

When every channel is busy, what happens depends on what you start:

| What you start | When all your channels are busy |
| :--- | :--- |
| [`POST /v2/calls`](/v2/calls#create-a-call) (phone, browser or text) | Refused with `429 concurrency_limit` and `Retry-After: 5`. No call is created and nothing is charged. |
| A [Realtime API](/v2/realtime) session | The WebSocket upgrade fails with `429 concurrency_limit` and `Retry-After: 5`. Sessions are not queued. |
| A [campaign](/v2/campaigns) | Not an error. The campaign never has more calls ringing or talking than you have channels, and dials the next contact when one frees up. |
| A scheduled callback | Waits for a channel, then dials. |
| An incoming call | Waits a few seconds for a channel. If none frees up, the call is not answered. |

**A call in progress is never cut** to make room, and never cut because your
channel count went down. A lower limit only stops new calls until enough have
ended.

```json title="429 Too Many Requests"
{
  "error": {
    "code": "concurrency_limit",
    "message": "concurrency limit reached: all 2 concurrent channels of this account are busy with calls and sessions; add channels in Billing or retry after 5 seconds"
  }
}
```

Honour `Retry-After` and retry with the same `Idempotency-Key`: a refused call
did not use the key. If you hit this often, you need more channels, or a
[campaign](/v2/campaigns), which paces itself to your channels for you.

Your channels are separate from the [request rate and queue
limits](/v2/limits). Those answer `429 rate_limited`; the channel limit answers
`429 concurrency_limit`.

## Add channels

Add channels in the console under **Billing → Plan tier → Concurrent channels →
Add channels**. Only owners and billing members can add them.

- Each extra channel costs the monthly price above, paid from your wallet
  credit when you add it. GST is charged when you add the credit, not again
  here.
- Channels you add together renew together, every month from the day you added
  them. Each renewal is paid from your wallet.
- **Remove** them at any time. They stay until the end of the month you paid
  for, and are not renewed.
- **If your wallet cannot cover a renewal**, those channels lapse at the end of
  their month: you are back to your included channels, nothing is owed, and
  calls in progress are not cut. We email your account's owners and billing
  members, and **Billing** shows the lapse. Add credit, then add the channels
  again.

Adding channels needs a [live account](/general/tiers#your-plan). Before you go
live, your workspace runs on its included channels.

## Accounts that were running before channels

If your account was already running calls when concurrent channels were
introduced, it keeps what it could already run, as included channels at no
charge: the most of its provisioned concurrency, its widest campaign and its
busiest moment in the previous 30 days. **Billing** shows your number.

## On your statement

Channel purchases and renewals are their own lines in your
[wallet transactions](/v2/wallet#a-concurrent-channels-row) (`kind:
"channels"`) and on your statement, separate from calls.
