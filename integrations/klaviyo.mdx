## Overview

The Klaviyo integration adds phone calls to your marketing campaigns. When you add a Voice Agent webhook node to a Klaviyo flow, you can reach customers in that flow with personalized voice calls, which improves engagement and conversion rates. On the call, the voice agent can deliver campaign messages, collect feedback and help customers directly.

## Setup Guide

### Step 1: Add Webhook Node in Klaviyo Flow

1. **Navigate to Your Klaviyo Flow**

   Open the Klaviyo flow where you want to add voice calls, or create a new one.

2. **Add Webhook Node**

   Click the "+" button to add a new action and select **Webhook** from the available options.

   ![Add webhook node](/images/klaviyo-integration/1-add-node.png)

### Step 2: Create Voice Agent Campaign with Webhook Support

3. **Access Voice Agents Dashboard**

   Navigate to the **Campaigns** section in your Voice Agents platform and create a new campaign.

4. **Enable Webhook Integration**

   When you create the campaign, enable webhook support. This lets Klaviyo send contact data directly to your voice agent campaign.

   ![Enable webhook integration](/images/klaviyo-integration/2-unable-webhook.png)

   <Tip>
   For complete campaign setup instructions, see our [Campaign Management Guide](/general/campaign).
   </Tip>

### Step 3: Copy Webhook Details

5. **Copy Webhook Endpoint**

   After you create the campaign with webhook support enabled, click the **"Copy Webhook"** button to open the webhook configuration modal.

   ![Copy webhook button](/images/klaviyo-integration/3-copy-webhook.png)

   The modal shows:
   - **Webhook URL**: the endpoint where Klaviyo sends data
   - **Secret Key**: used to authenticate requests between the two platforms

### Step 4: Configure Klaviyo Webhook

6. **Setup Webhook in Klaviyo**

   Return to your Klaviyo flow and configure the webhook node with the details from your Voice Agent campaign:

   - Paste the **Webhook URL** in the endpoint field
   - Add the **Secret Key** for authentication
   - Configure any additional payload data you want to send

   ![Setup webhook configuration](/images/klaviyo-integration/4-setup-webhook.png)

7. **Test and Activate**

   Test the webhook to check that data reaches Voice Agents from Klaviyo correctly, then activate your flow.

## What's Next?

Once your integration is complete, your Klaviyo flows can automatically trigger voice agent calls for:

- Abandoned cart recovery, to reach customers who left items in their cart
- Post-purchase follow-up, to collect feedback and encourage repeat purchases
- Replenishment reminders, to tell customers when it's time to reorder
- Win-back campaigns, to bring inactive customers back with personalized calls

The voice agent receives customer data from Klaviyo and can personalize the conversation using purchase history, preferences and behavior patterns.
